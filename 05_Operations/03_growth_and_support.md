# Growth, Search Acquisition, Retention and Customer Support

## Acquisition strategy
1. Local verified dealer/seller inventory and community partnerships, not national ads before supply.
2. High-quality SEO pages for real EV model/variant research (brand, model, city where inventory exists).
3. Editorial buying guides explaining battery SOH, claimed range, charging, comparison methodology and ownership cost.
4. Organic product demand via compatible accessory guides; avoid SEO pages with invented location-specific listings.
5. Partnerships with EV workshops/installers after due diligence, with clear referral disclosures.

## Conversion principles
One decisive CTA per page. Marketplace search as home hero intent; details page clear enquiry; sell wizard friction removal; accessory product confirm fit. Experiment consent-aware analytics; no dark patterns for contact permission.

## Retention
Saved searches, favourite EVs, price drop alerts where original asking prices are recorded, useful launch/ownership guides, order status, listing enquiry replies; notification preferences and unsubscribe controls.

## Service support
One intake system with issue category, ticket ID, owner, timestamps, status and user-visible next action. Escalate fraud allegations and privacy issues. For loan/insurance, direct underwriting/policy questions to regulated partner.

## Startup economic experiments
Interview dealer willingness to pay *after* observing leads. Calculate per-qualified-enquiry cost, accessory contribution margin and map/search/API cost per active user. Track paid campaigns vs organic leads by attributed cohort, never optimize just clicks.
