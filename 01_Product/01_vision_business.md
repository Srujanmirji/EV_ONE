# Vision, Positioning, Monetization, and Operating Model

## One-line pitch
EVONE is an India-first EV ecosystem connecting trustworthy used-vehicle discovery, new-model research, compatible accessories and everyday EV ownership information.

## Positioning
**Primary wedge:** transparent used EV discovery with trustworthy provenance and direct enquiries. **Acquisition layer:** searchable EV catalogue, comparisons, calculators, useful editorial. **Monetization layer:** first-party accessories, qualified dealer tools and, after approvals, carefully scoped partner referrals.

## Problems to validate (hypotheses, not findings)
1. Buyers distrust battery health and unverified seller claims.
2. Sellers/dealers struggle with low-quality leads and fragmented inventory tools.
3. EV shoppers have to reconcile OEM specs, independent road tests, prices and model variants from different sites.
4. Owners have trouble finding compatible accessories and current charging information.
5. Potential finance/insurance customers dislike opaque referral processes.

## Business model by stage
| Stage | Revenue hypothesis | Prerequisite | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | margin on first-party accessories | reliable inventory & returns | fulfilment cost |
| After pilot | dealer subscription and promoted listings | demonstrated lead quality | sellers reject fees |
| After validation | independent inspection referral fee | real trusted provider | misleading trust claims |
| Regulated partnership | finance/insurance referral revenue where legally permitted | approved contract/model & clear consent | compliance/consumer harm |
| Optional | tasteful sponsored editorial/ads | actual audience and disclosures | loss of neutrality |

**Pricing is NOT fixed.** Test willingness to pay via interviews and small experiments; do not treat proposed ₹ amounts as market facts. Separate sponsorship from editorial and rank integrity.

## Initial market
Pilot in one India region with dealer and individual seller supply (e.g. Hubli–Dharwad), then evaluate expansion to Bengaluru based on supply quality, consumer demand and operational capacity. Location examples do not claim a completed market study.

## Trust moat
Public provenance labels; verification only with evidentiary record; independent battery inspection partner; response-time tracking; anti-spam; clear claimed-vs-tested range; honest charging freshness; opt-in partner referrals.

## Competitive alternatives
General classified sites, dealer channels, OEM vehicle websites, accessories stores, charger-specific apps and automotive publications. Competitor research needed; do not assert market dominance.

## North star and unit economics
Qualified used EV enquiries per active verified listing per month. Track listing supply, enquiry-to-conversation, seller response time, moderation rejection, fraud reports, accessory gross margin after shipping/refunds, CAC by channel and partner lead consent conversion. Never count raw clicks as transactions.

## Important product boundary
EVONE does **not** guarantee used vehicle mechanical/battery condition, hold used-vehicle money or itself issue a loan/insurance policy in MVP. Terms must accurately describe the platform's role.
